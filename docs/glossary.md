# RCP Glossary

- **Provider** — system holding or producing relationship-related data.
- **Consumer** — system authorized to receive/use RCP context.
- **Relationship Context** — information relevant to a user's ongoing relationship with another person or organization.
- **Context Assertion** — a scoped statement exported through RCP, with provenance and policy metadata.
- **Capability** — what a provider can technically offer.
- **Permission** — whether a requested action may execute under current constraints.
- **Purpose** — declared reason for processing.
- **Provenance** — where an object came from.
- **Derivation** — transformation that produced one object from others.
- **Revocation** — withdrawal or invalidation of an authorization/policy basis.
- **Secure Envelope** — signed policy/routing metadata plus encrypted payload.
- **Control Plane** — capability/permission/policy coordination.
- **Data Plane** — context payload movement.
- **Trust Plane** — signatures, provenance, audit, attestation and conformance evidence.
